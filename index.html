Questions, answered

The things shop owners ask first.

What do you mean by "agentic"?
RevvOS isn't a dashboard you have to run. It's a fleet of AI agents that each own a job, answering the phone, chasing a quote, buying media, watching the books, and actually do the work, then hand you a finished draft to approve. You supervise a team instead of operating a tool.
Will the agents act without me seeing it?
Never, not until you decide they can. Every customer-facing message and money move starts as a draft you approve in one tap. As a specific agent proves itself with a clean track record, you can choose to let that one task run on its own. Anything about money or an upset customer always waits for a human.
Which platforms does it integrate with?
Google Ads, Meta Ads, Meta Business Suite, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster for marketing and reporting; Tekmetric and CCC ONE for shop management; Stripe and QuickBooks for finance; and HighLevel for CRM, SMS, and call logging. They power both your reporting and the agents that act through them.
Do I need to be technical to run it?
No. RevvOS is built for owners and service writers, not IT departments. Setup is a guided conversation, everything works on the phone in your hand, and it speaks plain shop English.
Where do the AI models actually run?
On-site. Every AI model runs locally on a dedicated machine that lives in your shop, not in a third-party cloud. Your customer data, call recordings, and financials are processed on that machine and are never sent out to be trained on, resold, or shared. It's a level of privacy most cloud tools simply can't offer.
Is my customer and financial data secure?
Yes. Because the models run on the machine in your shop, sensitive work like call transcription never leaves the building. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the United States, never resold or shared, and read-only by default on your systems of record.
How long does setup take, and how much work is it for me?
A complete, done-for-you install takes about 30 to 45 days, and it's almost entirely hands-off for you. Once you give us access to your accounts, our team handles the full build: standing up the in-shop machine, connecting every tool, and tuning the agents to how your shop runs. You're only ever asked to approve. There's no software for you to install and no training weekend.
