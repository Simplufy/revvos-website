The Missed-Call Catch: missed call text back for auto shops, set up in three steps

Missed call text back for auto shops works when three settings run together: a second phone rings when the front line is busy for 20 seconds, the caller gets a text within a minute saying who you are and when you'll call back, and one named person owns the callback list. That's the Missed-Call Catch. Below are the exact settings, three texts to copy, the callback rules, the compliance checks, and the four numbers to look at every Friday. No AI voice agent is needed.

Updated October 21, 2026 · 6 min read

Who this is for

This setup is for independent shops and multi-location groups whose phones ring while the advisor is under a hood, on another line or with a customer at the counter. It goes with our short video on the Missed-Call Catch and the longer article on missed call automation for auto repair.

The point of the video holds here too. You'd think the fix is a robot that answers every call, but if nobody owns the callbacks, a voice agent just creates more of them. Fix the catch first.

Why it matters: callers who reach nobody often try the next shop, and the phone is still where many bookings start. CDK's Service Shopper survey says 61% of customers call to schedule service. That's a dealership study, so your own share may differ; count it.

What you need first

  • A week of your call log. Your phone system shows missed and abandoned calls by hour. Write down missed calls per day, the busiest hours, and how many of those numbers called back or booked another way. Most shops find misses cluster around opening, lunch and close.
  • Admin access to your phone system (or the person who has it), to set the overflow ring.
  • A texting tool that can send an automatic reply to a missed call, and confirmation that its sending number is registered (more on that below).
  • A name. The person who will own the callback list.

The three settings, in order

1. The second ring

When the front line is busy or unanswered for 20 seconds, ring a second phone: the owner's cell, a tech's phone or a shared shop line. Most phone systems call this a ring group, hunt group or overflow rule.

Settings to use:

  • Ring time before overflow: 20 seconds.
  • Who rings second: one phone during busy hours, not the whole shop.
  • Hours: match your open hours; after hours, go straight to the text and voicemail.

2. The text within a minute

An automatic text goes to every caller you missed, within a minute. Keep it under three sentences: who you are, why you missed them and when they'll hear back. No prices, no promos, no booking link you haven't tested.

Three texts to copy (replace the shop name and times with yours):

During open hours:
Sorry we missed you at Redline Auto. We're with customers. Reply with what you need, or we'll call you back by 10.

Busy afternoon:
Sorry we missed your call at Redline Auto. Reply with your name and vehicle, and we'll call you back within the hour.

After hours:
Thanks for calling Redline Auto. We're closed for the night. Reply with what's going on with your vehicle and we'll call you first thing in the morning.

Pick a callback time you can keep. A promise of "by 10" that turns into noon is worse than no promise.

3. The owner and the clock

This is the step most shops skip, and it's where setups fall apart. The text arrives, the caller replies, and the reply sits in an inbox nobody opens.

  • One named person owns the list each day. Write it on the schedule.
  • Callbacks happen first thing at opening and through the busy hours, at the top of each hour.
  • If the owner is out, ownership moves with the schedule. Name the backup.
  • A callback is done when the customer is reached, or after two voicemails, not when someone means to call.

A simple callback list has five columns: number, time of the missed call, any text reply, who called back, and the result.

Compliance checks before you turn it on

Texts sent by software are regulated by the carriers. Twilio's documentation says anyone sending SMS or MMS from an application over a 10DLC number to the US must register for A2P 10DLC, so ask whichever tool sends your text whether that registration is done. A missed-call text replies to someone who just called you, but confirm the consent question with your own lawyer, and honor any stop reply right away.

If you ever add outbound calls with an AI voice, know that the FCC's February 2024 ruling treats AI-generated voices as artificial under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act. The Missed-Call Catch doesn't need any outbound AI calling.

The Friday check

Every Friday, write down four numbers for the week:

Number Where it comes from What it tells you
Missed calls Phone system call log Whether the second ring is set right
Texts sent Texting tool Whether every miss got a text
Replies received Texting tool Whether the text is worth answering
Callbacks completed Your callback list Whether the owner rule works

How to read it: if replies are coming in and callbacks aren't, the problem is staffing, not software. If texts go out and nobody replies, rewrite the text. If missed calls are still high in one hour, move the second ring for that hour.

A note on unknown numbers

The callback itself has a hurdle: many people ignore calls they don't recognize. Hiya's State of the Call 2026 reports that 86% of unknown calls go unanswered. That's one more reason the text goes first. When you call back, the customer has already seen your shop's name on their screen.

What this doesn't do

The Missed-Call Catch doesn't diagnose, quote or book by itself, and a text isn't a conversation. It also does nothing for calls you answered and then mishandled; that's training and a call sheet, which we cover in auto repair AI receptionist.

Questions people ask

What is the Missed-Call Catch?

Three settings that work together: a second phone rings after 20 seconds, the caller gets a text within a minute, and one named person owns the callback list.

Do I need an AI voice agent for missed calls?

No. If nobody owns the callbacks, a voice agent answers more calls and creates more callbacks. Set up the catch first and decide on a voice agent later.

What should a missed call text say?

Who you are, why you missed them and when they'll hear back, in under three sentences, with no prices or promos.

How do I know it's working?

Every Friday, compare missed calls, texts sent, replies received and callbacks completed. A gap between replies and callbacks points to staffing, not software.
