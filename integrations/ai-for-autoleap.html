Integration

If AutoLeap runs your shop, here is what sits above it.

AutoLeap is a complete shop management system, and it already includes AI call handling and reminder automation of its own. RevvOS does not replace any of that. It is the layer above your system of record that watches for exceptions across the phone, the estimates, and your marketing, and prepares the next step for a person to approve.

Two Layers, Two Jobs

The platform keeps the record. The layer moves the work.

A shop running AutoLeap has a strong system of record. The gap is not data. The gap is what happens between the moment something becomes an exception and the moment someone notices it.

Inside AutoLeap

The system of record

  • - Repair orders, estimates, and invoices
  • - Digital vehicle inspections with photo and video markup
  • - Scheduling board and technician dispatching
  • - Parts procurement and catalog lookup
  • - Customer messaging, reminders, and review collection
  • - QuickBooks sync and payment processing
  • - AI call handling for initial phone capture
Above AutoLeap

The system of action

  • + One exception queue across every system, not one per system
  • + A declined line tracked as a state until it is resolved
  • + A missed call connected to the RO and history behind it
  • + Ad and search performance read next to the work it produced
  • + A prepared next step, drafted and waiting for approval
  • + A record of what was approved, edited, or declined

AutoLeap capabilities listed above reflect the platform's own published positioning. Confirm current features directly with AutoLeap.

Where The Two Meet

Four places the layers touch.

These are the moments where a record system has the data and an operating layer turns it into action.

Declined work
AutoLeap holds the declined line on the RO. The layer holds it as a state, decides when it is worth re-approaching, and drafts the message so the advisor is not remembering a brake job from three weeks ago. Declined work follow-up ->
The phone
AutoLeap captures incoming calls. The layer connects what happened on that call to the customer's history and the open work, so a caller who was already a customer is treated like one, and a missed call is recovered rather than logged. Missed call recovery ->
Estimates
AutoLeap stores the estimate and its approval state. The layer watches the estimate by state (sent, seen, approved not scheduled, partially approved) and prompts the right next action for that state. Estimate follow-up ->
Marketing
AutoLeap runs reminders and review collection. The layer reads the demand side (local search, ads, reviews) next to the booking side, so the shop can see which spend produced work and which calls went unanswered. Marketing and demand ->

How The Connection Works

Your data stays where it lives.

RevvOS reads from AutoLeap and writes back to it. The repair order stays the repair order. The customer record stays the customer record. An advisor does not learn a second interface to do their job, and nothing is double-entered.

The exact shape of any connection (which fields are read, how often, and what is written back) depends on the platform's current API and your configuration, so we confirm it against your setup rather than describing a promise. What we will not do is claim a specific sync behavior on a page and then deliver something narrower.
