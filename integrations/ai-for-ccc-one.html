Integration

CCC ONE governs the estimate. Something still has to govern the week.

CCC ONE, the Total Repair Platform, is where a collision job is written, estimated, and tracked through repair workflow, and it sits inside a network that connects insurers, repairers, OEMs, and parts suppliers. That is a strong system for the claim. It is not a system for the customer who has called three times, or the supplement that stalled a bay for nine days.

For Multi-Shop Operators

Cycle time is a portfolio number, not a shop number.

An MSO running several collision locations lives and dies by cycle time across the whole portfolio. CCC ONE tracks each shop's jobs. The operating layer answers a different question: which location is drifting this week, which is blocked on supplements, and which is losing jobs at the phone, before the monthly report tells you it already happened.

The layer reads from CCC ONE and writes back to it. The estimate stays the estimate, the workflow stays the workflow, and nothing is double-entered. The exact connection depends on CCC's current interfaces and your configuration, which we confirm against your setup. Multi-location operations ->
