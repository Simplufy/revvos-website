Integration

Mitchell 1 is not one system. It is three.

Most shops that run Mitchell 1 mean one of three things: ProDemand for repair information, Manager SE for shop management, or SocialCRM for marketing. Each is strong at its own job. The operating layer matters most where those three do not meet: the technician's finding, the advisor's follow-up, and the customer's decision happen in different places.

The Three Mitchell 1 Systems

Know which one you actually run.

A "Mitchell 1 integration" means something different depending on which product the shop has. Here is what each does, in Mitchell 1's own framing.

PRODUCT 01

ProDemand

Repair information and diagnostics

OEM repair procedures, diagnostics, labor estimating, and verified troubleshooting. This is where the technician decides what the vehicle needs.

PRODUCT 02

Manager SE

Shop management

Daily business workflow and administration: repair orders, estimates, the front counter. This is where the job is recorded and invoiced.

PRODUCT 03

SocialCRM

Marketing and retention

Automated marketing and retention: website, LocalSearch SEO, and targeted Google Ads. This is where the shop reaches customers between visits.

Product names and framing reflect Mitchell 1's own published material, October 2026. Mitchell 1 describes itself as a member of the Snap-on family of brands. Confirm current products directly with Mitchell 1.

The Space Between

Data crosses the three. Accountability does not.

ProDemand knows what the vehicle needs. Manager SE knows what the customer approved. SocialCRM knows what campaigns went out. Nothing is positioned to hold all three against one another and ask whether the customer ever said yes.

ProDemand finds the work. A fault, a maintenance item, a recommendation the technician documents.
->
The layer asks: did it reach the customer? A finding that never becomes a presented, followed-up line is a finding that never had a chance.
Manager SE holds the estimate. Approved, declined, partially approved, waiting.
->
The layer asks: is the declined line still open? Declined is a state, not a verdict. The layer keeps it alive and drafts the next approach.
SocialCRM sends the campaigns. Website, local search, Google Ads, retention messaging.
->
The layer asks: did the spend produce answered calls? Campaign performance read next to the phone and the booked work, not in isolation.

That connective work is the operating layer: declined work follow-up, missed call recovery, and demand performance held in one queue.

Boundaries

RevvOS does not try to be your repair information.

ProDemand's diagnostic content is a large, licensed, continuously updated body of repair knowledge. Nobody should replace that with a general AI. The same is true of Manager SE's records. RevvOS reads from the systems you already trust and writes back to them, so the estimate stays the estimate and the technician keeps their tools.
