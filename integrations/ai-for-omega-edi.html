Integration

A glass job runs on two tracks. Omega EDI runs the one that gets paid.

Omega EDI is auto glass business management software: the system that carries a job from dispatch to insurance billing. "EDI" here is the electronic claim channel that glass shops use with insurers. The service track and the money track are not the same track, and most glass shops lose margin in the gap between them.

Name check

"EDI" is a technology. Omega EDI is an auto glass product.

Electronic Data Interchange is a general way of moving claim and billing data between systems. You will also find a healthcare EDI and other same-named tools in search results. This page is about Omega EDI, the auto glass business management software used by vehicle glass shops for repair, replacement, calibration, and insurance billing. If you run a glass bay, you are in the right place.

The Two Tracks

Glass gets fixed on one track. Money arrives on another.

The vehicle is done when the glass is in and calibrated. The job is not done until the claim is billed and paid. Those two finish lines are on different clocks, and the shop feels the difference at the end of the month.

Track A - Service

Fix the vehicle

01  Book the job and identify the glass by VIN
02  Source the part and confirm availability
03  Dispatch a mobile tech or a bay
04  Install, and calibrate where the vehicle needs it
05  Return the vehicle

Omega EDI carries this track: the job, the tech, the vehicle, the route.

Track B - Money

Get paid for it

01  Determine insurer or customer pay, and the deductible
02  File the claim through the EDI channel
03  Wait for adjudication and any shortfall
04  Invoice and collect the balance
05  Reconcile what was billed against what landed

Omega EDI carries this track too: the claim, the billing, the receivables.

The gap is the handoff.

A job can be complete on Track A and stuck on Track B for weeks, and nothing on either track treats that as an exception worth escalating. The operating layer watches the handoff: a claim filed but not adjudicated, a balance billed but not collected, a job closed but not paid, and drafts the next step for a person to approve.

Boundaries

Omega EDI stays the system of record for the glass job.

RevvOS does not replace dispatch, parts sourcing, or claim filing. It reads from Omega EDI and writes back to it, so the job stays the job and the tech keeps their tools.
