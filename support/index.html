What to include

Three details help us start faster.

Plain language is best. Screenshots are useful when they do not contain sensitive customer information.

01

Where you saw it

Name the shop, page, workflow, or work item involved.

02

What you expected

Tell us what you thought should happen, even if you are not sure.

03

What happened instead

Include when it began and whether the problem is still happening.

Please do not email passwords, API keys, payment details, or unnecessary customer information. We will ask for a safer way to share sensitive evidence if it is needed.

Straight answers

Before you send a message.

Who reads support messages?

A person on the RevvOS team reviews the request and routes it to whoever can investigate or answer it.

Do I need a ticket number?

No. Start with the shop or workflow involved. If a ticket number is useful, we will provide one in the reply.

Can I send customer information?

Only include what is necessary and permitted by your business. Do not send passwords, payment details, API keys, or full customer records by email.

What if I am not a current customer?

Use the same email for a product question, or book a demo if you want to explore whether RevvOS fits your shops.

Start with the sentence you would say to a coworker.

“This list changed,” “I cannot get in,” or “I do not understand this item” is enough to begin.
