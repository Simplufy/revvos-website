The Real Win Check: three settings for Google Ads for auto repair shops

The Real Win Check is a setup for Google Ads for auto repair shops that tells the ad AI what a real win is before it spends more money. Count a phone call above a minimum length as the conversion, keep your shop name in its own exact match Search campaign, and read every landing page and generated headline the ads can use. The steps, the account note to copy and a monthly check are below.

Updated October 21, 2026 · 7 min read

The Real Win Check is three settings to fix before an auto repair shop lets Google's ad AI spend more money. They are the same three as the video, in the same order:

  1. Count a phone call as the win, with a minimum call length so hang-ups don't count.
  2. Keep your shop name in its own exact match campaign, because Google serves it ahead of Performance Max.
  3. Read every page your ads land on, including the headlines Google generates, because what they claim is still yours.

Below is the full setup for each one, what to watch out for, and a monthly check that tells you whether the ads are producing repair orders or just clicks.

Why the AI needs a real win

Most of the "AI" in Google Ads is automated bidding and Performance Max. Google describes Performance Max as a goal-based campaign type: you give it creative assets, conversion goals, audience signals and a budget, and its system decides where and when to show your ads. Google's own Performance Max overview sells it as one campaign across its channels.

That is the whole problem in one sentence. The system works toward the goal you give it. If the goal is a page view or a form fill that rarely turns into a booked job, it will find you more page views and form fills. A repair shop's real win is usually a phone call that turns into an appointment, and the AI can't know that unless you tell it. Our longer page on AI Google Ads for auto repair covers the background.

Who this is for

  • Independent shops running their own Google Ads account, or paying someone who does.
  • Multi-shop groups where each location has its own phone number and its own ads.
  • Anyone about to switch on Performance Max, or raise its budget, who hasn't looked at the conversion settings in a while.

What you need first

  • Admin access to the Google Ads account (not just a monthly report).
  • A list of every phone number your shop uses: the main line, each location, and any tracking numbers.
  • A list of the services you want and don't want (for example, no transmissions or no diesel).
  • Your shop system's repair order count by month, so you can compare it with what the ads report.

Step 1. Make a phone call the win

Google defines a conversion action as "a specific customer activity that is valuable to your business." For most shops that activity is a call. Google Ads can count calls straight from your ads, calls to a phone number on your website and clicks on a phone number on your mobile site, as its conversions help page lists.

Set it up like this:

  1. In Google Ads, create a phone call conversion action for calls from your ads and one for calls to the number on your website.
  2. For website calls, set a call length. Google lets you count a call only when it lasts longer than a minimum length you choose, which filters out hang-ups and wrong numbers. Pick a length that matches a real booking conversation at your counter.
  3. Make the call actions your primary goal, and demote anything that isn't a customer (page views, scrolls, time on site) to secondary so the bidding ignores it.
  4. Check the cautions on Google's page: its website call tag tracks one number per page by default, and some third-party call tracking can stop Google from verifying the number.

Copy this note into your account notes: "Primary goal: calls from ads and website calls over our minimum length. Forms count only if they book an appointment. Reviewed on (date) by (name)."

Step 2. Protect your shop name

People who search for your shop by name were already coming to you. You don't want the AI to take credit for them, or to pay a premium for them inside a broad campaign. Google says a Search campaign with an exact match keyword is prioritized to serve over Performance Max when the search matches that keyword exactly.

  1. Create a small Search campaign called "Brand" with exact match keywords for your shop name and its common misspellings, for example [redline auto] and [red line auto] (demo shop).
  2. In Performance Max, add brand exclusions for your own name so it doesn't chase searches the Brand campaign already wins.
  3. Add negative keywords for work you don't do. Google's guide to building a keyword list explains match types if you need a refresher.
  4. Keep the Brand budget small and separate, so you can see its calls on their own line.

Step 3. Read every page and headline

Performance Max can use final URL expansion, which lets Google send people to whichever page on your site it thinks fits the search. Google's note is direct: "you remain responsible for reviewing and ensuring compliance and accuracy of landing page content", and that includes the assets it generates.

  1. List every page the ads can reach. If an old promo page or a service you dropped is still live, exclude it or take it down.
  2. Read each generated headline and description. Remove anything you can't back up, such as "Same-day service" or "Lifetime warranty" if those aren't your real terms.
  3. Check that each landing page shows a phone number that is tracked by step 1.
  4. Repeat this whenever you add a page to the site.

What to watch out for

  • Don't scale before the calls are counted right. Raising the budget on a bad goal buys more of the wrong thing faster.
  • Make sure the phone gets answered. An ad that produces a call nobody picks up is the most expensive kind of waste. Our pages on AI phone answering for auto repair and missed call automation cover that side.
  • Expect the click to check your reviews. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found that 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, so a weak profile can undo a good ad.
  • Keep prices and offers human. Only put an offer in ad text that your counter will honor.

How to tell it worked

Once a month, put three numbers side by side for each location: calls the ads reported, appointments your shop system booked from those calls, and repair orders that came out of them. If ad calls go up and repair orders don't, the win is still wrong, or the phone isn't being answered.

Also remember that the Business Profile counts something different. Its calls metric counts the times a customer tapped the call button on your profile, so it won't match your ad call count, and it shouldn't.

Questions people ask

Is Performance Max good for auto repair shops?

It can be, once it chases the right goal. Performance Max works toward the conversion goals you set, so a shop should make real phone calls the primary goal and keep its own name in a separate exact match Search campaign first.

What should count as a conversion for a repair shop?

Usually a phone call long enough to be a real conversation, plus any form that books an appointment. Page views and time on site aren't customers, so set them as secondary.

Why do my ad calls not match my Google Business Profile calls?

They count different things. The profile counts taps on its call button, while the ad account counts the call conversions you set up, so the two numbers are not supposed to match.

Can I trust the headlines Google writes for my ads?

Read them first. Google says you remain responsible for landing page content and generated assets, so remove any claim your shop can't back up.
