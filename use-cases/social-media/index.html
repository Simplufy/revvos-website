Who checks a sensitive automotive post?

A person chosen by your business reviews it. Customer vehicles, repair claims, safety guidance, offers, and partnerships can receive an extra check.

Does RevvOS publish the post?

Publishing stays manual unless your business has confirmed a supported connection and its approval steps. Ready to schedule does not mean the post was sent.

How do shop teams share story material?

Use the photos, notes, and files your team already creates. We will shape the collection process around how your locations work.
