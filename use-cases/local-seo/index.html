Questions from location owners

Does RevvOS change our public information by itself?

No. RevvOS organizes a recommendation beside the current source. A person reviews it before an authorized team member changes a profile, directory, or page.

Can each automotive location confirm its own hours?

The question can go to the location manager who knows the schedule, while the brand team keeps the review process it requires.

How do we know what was checked?

Each item shows the check date, original page or profile, suggested change, reviewer, and final live result.
