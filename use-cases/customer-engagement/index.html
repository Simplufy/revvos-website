Questions at the handoff

Does RevvOS send estimate follow-ups without a person?

No. RevvOS can prepare a draft and route it to a service advisor for review. An authorized person decides whether and how to contact the customer.

Does this include every customer interaction?

It includes the calls, texts, forms, estimates, and notes connected to the follow-up your team is reviewing.

Can the follow-up path change?

Yes. A service advisor can review a nudge, reframe the estimate, or decide that a final call is more appropriate. The person remains responsible for the outreach.
