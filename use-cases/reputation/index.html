Questions in the margin

Know exactly what RevvOS handles.

Q/01

What does RevvOS show?

Ratings and low-star reviews by shop, plus a prepared reply for your team to review.

Q/02

Does RevvOS post natively to a Business Profile?

No. A person approves the reply and posts it through the business's chosen review channel.

Q/03

Does the handoff mean the issue is fixed?

No. RevvOS gets the concern to a manager, but the case is not complete until the shop confirms what happened next.
