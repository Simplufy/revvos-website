Useful answers

What managers ask before looking closer.

Is this another dashboard?

It is a working view across the shop systems you already use. It connects a problem to a clear next step and the person responsible.

Can a manager look closely without changing the repair order?

Yes. A manager can see the original system, last update, time in stage, and recommended follow-up without RevvOS moving the repair order.

What happens when data is missing?

RevvOS shows unavailable rather than guessing a value. A person reviews the available context and decides what happens next.
