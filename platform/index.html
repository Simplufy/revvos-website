Practical questions

How RevvOS fits into your shop.

Does RevvOS replace our shop systems?

No. It works alongside the shop, customer, finance, and marketing systems you already use.

What happens when someone approves a draft?

RevvOS records the decision. Sending or updating another system only happens when that workflow has been set up for your business.

How current is the information?

Every item shows where it came from and when it was last updated. If information is missing, RevvOS says so.
