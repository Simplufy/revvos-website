How it works

The decision first. The details when you need them.

Notes for the reader

Is this another shop management system?

No. It gives you a clear view across the systems your shops already use. The actual work stays in those systems.

Can I see where an item came from?

Yes. Each item shows the original system and when it was last updated. Missing data is clearly marked.

Does RevvOS make the decision or contact the customer?

RevvOS can prepare the draft or next step, but a person reviews it. Sending only happens when your business has set up that workflow.
