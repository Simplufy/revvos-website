How RevvOS fits into the workday.

Is RevvOS the shop's primary dispatch system?

No. It gives your team a clear view across the shop and customer systems you already use.

What goes into an advisor or manager handoff?

The original system, last update, current issue, person responsible, and recommended next step.

Does approval automatically contact a customer or update a repair order?

Only when that workflow has been set up for your business. Otherwise, approval records the decision for your team.
