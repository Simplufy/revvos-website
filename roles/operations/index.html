Straight answers

Straight answers for operations leaders.

Does this replace the shop system?
No. The Job Board works alongside Tekmetric, CCC ONE, Urable, or the booking system you already use.
What appears in Needs You Now?
Exceptions such as stale estimates, aging repair orders, overdue parts, callbacks owed, and pickup-ready vehicles.
Does the preview update a repair order?
No. It demonstrates a possible board state without changing the source record.
