Owner questions

What belongs in the owner view?

Do I need to review every shop item?

No. The morning brief only raises work that needs an owner's decision or review. Routine work stays with the shop team.

How current is the owner view?

Each item shows its original system and last update. If a connection is down, RevvOS marks the missing information instead of guessing.

Does approval update the shop system or contact a customer?

Only when that specific workflow has been set up. Otherwise, approval records your decision and sends it to the right person.
