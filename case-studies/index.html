Stale estimate recovery

Situation

A service estimate remains open after the customer leaves without authorizing work. The shop-management system shows no documented follow-up owner or next-contact date.

Next step

The service advisor reviews the estimate history and gets the right follow-up ready for a manager.

Common exception

The estimate includes a declined safety item, but the customer's preferred contact method is not recorded.

Questions to ask

  • When should an open estimate enter the recovery queue?
  • Which source-system details must the advisor review?
  • Who reviews the prepared action before contact?
File 02 / Fictional discussion copyREPRESENTATIVE AUTOMOTIVE SCENARIO

11-day aging collision RO

Situation

A collision repair order has been open for 11 days. The source system shows work in progress, while the latest note does not identify the blocking step or current owner.

Next step

The operations lead reviews the latest record and sends a clear next step to the shop manager.

Common exception

A supplement is awaiting insurer review, but that status only appears in a separate note.

Questions to ask

  • Which event starts aging review?
  • What evidence identifies the real blocker?
  • Who reviews and owns the next action?
File 03 / Fictional discussion copyREPRESENTATIVE AUTOMOTIVE SCENARIO

Overdue part

Situation

A repair order is waiting on a part beyond its expected date. The parts record has a purchase reference but no current arrival date or customer-update owner.

Next step

The parts lead checks with the supplier and prepares an update for the service advisor and shop manager.

Common exception

The supplier shipped a partial order, but the source system still shows the original expected date.

Questions to ask

  • When is a part considered overdue?
  • Which feed provides the authoritative status?
  • Who reviews the customer-facing next step?
